Travel to Egypt

Pyramids of Giza — one of the most famous monuments of Ancient Egypt, built over 4,500 years ago. They served as tombs for pharaohs and continue to amaze people with their massive size, precise construction, and historical mystery.

Sahara Desert — the largest hot desert in the world, stretching across much of North Africa. It is famous for its vast golden sand dunes, rocky plateaus, and extreme climate, making it both harsh and stunningly beautiful.
