Snickers Cheesecake Bars

  • Time: total time 4 hours 15 minutes
  • Serves: 6 person
Ingredients Quantity
Oreo® cookies 1 package
Melted butter 1/4 cup
Sugar 1/3 cup
Cream cheese 2 packages
Large egg 1
Sour cream 1/3 cup
Vanilla extract 1 teaspoon
Salt 1/4 teaspoon
Snickers® 6

Step 1: Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Generously line a 9x9-inch baking pan with enough aluminum foil to have overhang on 2 sides. Grease with cooking spray.

Step 2: Place cookies in a food processor and pulse until finely ground. You should have 2 3/4 cups crumbs. Add melted butter and pulse until combined. Spread onto the bottom of the prepared baking pan.

Step 3: Bake in the preheated oven for 8 to 10 minutes. Remove from oven, but keep oven on.

Step 4: Meanwhile, beat cream cheese, sugar, sour cream, egg, vanilla, and salt in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth.

Step 5: Set aside 1/2 cup of the chopped candy bars. Fold remaining chopped candy bars into the cream cheese mixture.

Step 6: Spread filling mixture over the crust.

Step 7: Bake in the preheated oven until the edges are browned and set, 35 to 40 minutes.

Pizza with Mushroom

  • Time: total time 25 minutes
  • Serves: 3 person
Ingredients Quantity
Pre-baked pizza 1
Olive oil 3 tablespoons
Sesame oil 1 teaspoon
Fresh spinach 1 cup
Mozzarella cheese 8 ounces
Mushrooms 1 cup sliced

Step 1: Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Place pizza crust on a baking sheet.

Step 2: In a small bowl, mix together olive oil and sesame oil. Brush onto pre-baked pizza crust, covering the entire surface. Stack spinach leaves, then cut lengthwise into 1/2-inch strips; scatter evenly over crust. Cover pizza with shredded mozzarella, and top with sliced mushrooms.

Step 3: Bake in the preheated oven for 8 to 10 minutes, or until cheese is melted and edges are crisp.

Piña Colada Cocktail

  • Time: total time 5 minutes
  • Serves: 1 person
Ingredients Quantity
coconut milk 1 cup
pineapple juice 1 cup
white sugar 4 tablespoons
ice cubes 8

Step 1: Combine coconut milk, pineapple juice, sugar, and ice in a blender. Blend until smooth. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.
