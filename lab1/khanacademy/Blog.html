First post

Why exactly this book?

The book made me think about how our choices shape who we become and how difficult it can be to return to something we once left behind.

Main idea:

This novel is about love, fate, and second chances. The story connects the past and the present, showing how one moment can change an entire life.

Second post

Why exactly this film?

The film made me question what is real and how our thoughts can shape reality. It shows how deeply emotions and memories can affect our decisions.

Main idea:

A thief who steals corporate secrets through the use of dream-sharing technology is given the inverse task of planting an idea into the mind of a CEO, but his tragic past may doom the project and his team to disaster.
